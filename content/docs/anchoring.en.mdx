title: On-chain proof and independent verification
description: Version digests are written to Aptos mainnet, and checking one calls no Re0 endpoint.
---

Published versions, daily audit chain heads and closed publisher settlements have their digests written to **Aptos mainnet**.

**Digests only**: no knowledge text, no queries, no user identifiers, no assets.

## What it does and does not prove

<Callout type="warn">
A proof establishes that "this exact content existed at this moment". It is **not a guarantee that the content is correct**. For a settlement it proves only that the figures were not rewritten after anchoring, never that the allocation was computed correctly.
</Callout>

## When the chain is unavailable

If the chain or the signing account is unreachable, publishing, refreshing, retrieval, metering, review and billing are **all unaffected**; the proof simply stays pending.

Anchoring consumes no call quota and is included on both Free and Pro. No wallet, no signature and no gas is involved for you at any point.

## Verifying it yourself

The verifier is published separately and open. It depends only on on-chain data and the cited content you already hold — it **calls no Re0 endpoint**.
